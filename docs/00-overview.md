# Overview
Amazing Goats (Ezimbuzini), a specialised agricultural division of Besbpo Group, is building a **digital agricultural produce orchestration and fulfilment platform**.

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| Orchestrate | Demand aggregation, buying plans, partner farms, abattoirs, co-packers, quality and traceability |
| Fulfil | Cut-off cycles, batches, cold-chain routes, delivery slots, proof of delivery |
| Sell | Public store, trade portal, subscriptions, merchandising |
| Monetise | Ezimbuzini Ads, data, embedded finance |

Product range: 16 lines, about 140 products (`docs/catalogue-v1.xlsx`), phased 1 to 3. Lamb is the flagship; chicken, eggs, beef, offal, sausages, mince, biltong, seasonal poultry, sundries, pork, dairy and produce follow. Live goats and indigenous chickens are sold on the farm.

Key business rules: prices exclude VAT; chilled products are pre-ordered by cut-off cycle; trade clients buy on account with tiered credit; abattoirs, farms and co-packers are partners.
